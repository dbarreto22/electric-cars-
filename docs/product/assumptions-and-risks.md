# Assumptions and risks

Statuses describe what is known at this discovery stage: **confirmed** means directly established by the project brief or repository inspection, **assumed** means a working hypothesis to validate, and **unresolved** means a decision or evidence is still needed. No market statistics, vehicle specifications, prices, or legal conclusions are asserted here.

| Area | Assumption or risk | Status | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | People in Uruguay considering a new, fully electric car within 12 months are a useful initial audience. | Assumed | Check whether staging visitors fit this description and find the journey relevant. |
| Product | Budget, driving patterns, passenger/space needs, and charging access are enough to start matching needs. | Assumed | Watch for skipped questions, poor matches, and missing decision factors. |
| Product | A short list of up to three explained options is more useful than a broad catalog. | Assumed | Gather usefulness and clarity feedback; observe result engagement. |
| Product | Visitors will trust recommendations when reasons and source dates are visible. | Assumed | Ask directly about source trust and inspect reports of confusing claims. |
| Data | A small catalog can be manually verified and kept current at MVP scale. | Assumed | Identify authoritative sources, per-field provenance, review cadence, and an owner. |
| Data | Sufficient reliable information can be found for each field needed to compare candidates. | Unresolved | Inventory sources and fields before finalizing questionnaire or matching rules. |
| Data | Source links and last-checked dates communicate freshness clearly. | Assumed | Test whether visitors can find and interpret them. |
| Data | Incorrect or changed vehicle information can be triaged and corrected promptly. | Unresolved | Define intake, verification, correction, and removal workflow and ownership. |
| Technical | The repository currently contains empty `Front` and `Back` directories and no visible project instructions or application files. | Confirmed | Inspect the intended stack and deployment constraints during technical design. |
| Technical | AI may help interpret answers or explain results without inventing facts. | Assumed | Keep factual claims grounded in verified catalog fields and evaluate explanations before use. |
| Technical | A particular framework, data store, hosting service, or AI provider is appropriate. | Unresolved | Decide after inspecting requirements and existing environment; none is selected here. |
| Operational | Someone can maintain the catalog and answer correction reports. | Unresolved | Assign an owner and define a sustainable update process before production. |
| Operational | Staging visitors can be reached without scheduled interviews. | Assumed | Identify low-effort channels and recruit a small voluntary feedback group. |
| Operational | Usage and feedback can be measured with minimal personal data. | Assumed | Choose aggregate events and retention limits; avoid collecting identity unless needed. |
| Operational | Staging feedback can improve usability and recommendations but cannot alone demonstrate commercial demand. | Confirmed | Treat it as formative evidence and seek separate evidence before business decisions. |
