## Matching readiness

| Field | Use now? | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Fully electric eligibility | **Limited use** | The BYD product pages explicitly describe the named products as electric/100% electric. Suitable only to mark a model-page candidate as BEV pending exact trim confirmation; not enough by itself to publish an exact version. |
| Published range | **Display-only for now** | Preserve 397/380 km with NEDC label and source date. Do not infer real-world trip fit or assign the family-level Yuan Pro range to GS/GS X. Use in matching only after version/year is confirmed and cycle policy is defined. |
| Passenger capacity | **Omit** | No unambiguous, trim-specific seat count is verified. Confirm directly from exact local ficha or importer. |
| Cargo/space | **Display-only for Yuan Pro family** | Its page gives cargo volume, but trim/year applicability is not mapped. Other two have qualitative space claims only. Do not score. |
| Battery, power, connector, charge time | **Display-only for named product page** | Claims are useful to show with source and caveat after exact version mapping. AC charging power is absent; charge time depends on equipment/context. Do not rank charging fit yet. |
| Price / promotion | **Dated observation only** | Current pages show “desde” values, but no exact trim applicability or validity. Do not use as budget eligibility, quote, or guaranteed current price. Reconfirm with importer and preserve observed date. |
| Availability / model year / warranty | **Omit** | No current stock, delivery, model year, or warranty applicability established. |

As of this check, the catalog is **not sufficient to drive production-style questionnaire matching** on budget, passenger/space needs, driving range, and charging. It can support a transparent prototype shell or source-review exercise, but should return “insufficient verified version data” rather than rank these candidates.

## Decisions needed before the app uses it

1. Ask the BYD Uruguay importer for current exact trim names, applicable model years, and trim-specific technical sheets for NEW E2 X, SEAGULL SURF, and YUAN PRO GS/GS X; confirm which products are currently offered for new-car purchase.
2. Confirm seats, cargo (including measurement method), battery gross/usable capacity, range cycle and trim mapping, AC/DC connector and power, and any other fields selected for matching against a local ficha or written importer response.
3. Decide what evidence qualifies as “available in Uruguay” (local product listing, current written confirmation, or other threshold) and recheck it on a defined cadence. A listing or reservation button is not stock confirmation.
4. Obtain current price and promotion terms by exact trim, currency, observation date, expiry/validity, and included items if price will affect matching. Otherwise omit price-based matching at launch.
5. Assign an accountable catalog owner and reviewer, correction path, stale thresholds, and a change-history/editing workflow. Confirm what source material can be stored or quoted; reuse permission has not been established.
6. Identify additional primary local sources beyond BYD if the catalog should include more than these families. Do not add Renault model names based only on the broad E-Tech page.

**Readiness decision:** no exact candidate version is yet complete enough for the intended results flow. The strongest leads are BYD SEAGULL SURF (best visible connector/battery/range evidence) and BYD YUAN PRO (local GS/GS X labels plus space/range claims, but ambiguous version linkage); NEW E2 X is another plausible lead with a published NEDC range. All need trim/year and passenger-capacity confirmation before ranking.
