# Catalog validation backlog

## Purpose and review rules

This is a normalized review queue derived from `docs/data/research/brand-model-inventory.xlsx`, not a verified product catalog. The workbook was read without editing it. Its `Modelos 100% Eléctricos (EV)` column contains **75 entries**; the column heading is user-supplied and does not prove drivetrain or Uruguay availability. Two entries in that column are verified as non-BEVs and are kept in the separate out-of-scope table below. The workbook's separate hybrid column is not promoted into the BEV list; its labels are also user-supplied and require their own validation.

All linked workbook sources below were checked on **2026-10-07**. Where a publisher date was visible and material, it is called out in the note. Most pages do not show a publication date. “Official source supports model” refers to a Uruguay-specific official page that explicitly names the exact model or named version; an official page for another country or a broken/unavailable page does not count. `confirmed_for_sale` means a current local sales page presents a price, quote, reservation, or purchase contact; it does **not** confirm stock or delivery. Prices are dated observations only. `announced` is reserved for an explicit Uruguay announcement of a future offer; none is assigned from a model page alone.

The table preserves workbook names as supplied (minor display-only whitespace trimming). Each source link is the workbook URL; `—` means the workbook cell was blank. Provenance uses only `user_supplied`, `official_local_source`, or `secondary_source`. No secondary source was needed for these checks.

## Evidence notes and use limits

- Some regional sites serve the wrong market despite a `.com.uy` URL. The checked BMW iX1/iX2/i4/i5 pages included BMW Costa Rica branding or Costa Rican sales links; Audi A6 Sportback e-tron and RS e-tron GT Performance pages were likewise branded for Costa Rica. These are not Uruguay offer sources.
- The Jeep Avenger URL resolves to a Jeep UK product page despite its `/uy/` path. It supports that the global model is a BEV, not an Uruguay offer; it is therefore recorded as `not_found` for local market support.
- A product page and current quote/reserve route support `confirmed_for_sale` as a market-listing status, not an inventory guarantee. For Chevrolet Spark EUV, Equinox EV, and Captiva EV, displayed price terms were valid through 2026-09-30 and are expired as of the check date; do not reuse those prices as current.
- “Desde” prices or prices with no stated validity are only observations made on 2026-10-07. They are not guaranteed current quotes. Some pages display `$` without identifying currency; do not assume USD.
- Range claims use varied cycles (NEDC, WLTP, CLTC, EPA, or city-only claims). Store the exact cycle and version applicability; do not compare unlike cycles or treat urban claims without a named protocol as homologated range.
- A page-level fact is not automatically version-level evidence. Several pages name multiple versions but do not bind every specification to a trim. No model year was established for most entries.
- `secondary_source` is an allowed provenance value but was not used in this pass. Autoblog Uruguay was not needed to resolve these official page checks; its reports should remain secondary evidence if used in a later pass.

## Fields that can be considered for a later pilot

This backlog is not ready to drive recommendations. Candidates with current local product pages, named BEV versions, and some technical fields worth taking into a **verification pilot** include Geely EX2/EX5, Leapmotor T03/B10/C10 EV/C11, Peugeot e-2008, Wuling Mini EV/Binguo EV/Bingo S, and the Riddara RD6/Horizon versions. Chevrolet, BYD and MG have broad local official offers too, but trim-level mappings or stale/ambiguous price and range claims need review. These are not rankings or approved recommendation candidates.

Before any candidate is served to users, a catalog owner should obtain or verify an exact local trim and model year, seating/cargo configuration, range and test cycle, battery/charging facts used by the question flow, and current market status. Confirm prices by exact trim with currency, effective date/expiry and inclusions; otherwise omit budget-based matching. Establish source reuse/quotation policy and who reviews corrections. Do not imply recommendations are supported until these checks are complete.
