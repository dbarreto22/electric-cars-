# Verified pilot catalog

**Source review checked:** 2026-10-07. This is a narrow evidence pilot, not a recommendation list and not an approved application catalog.

## Selection method

Candidates were retained for verification only where Uruguay-specific manufacturer/importer material identifies a fully electric local version, names a model year, and shows a local purchase or reservation route in its latest accessible content. Coverage considers different space and range profiles. No fit or ranking is implied. Facts below are limited to those stated on the sources; blank evidence is not inferred from another trim or market. “Checked” is the date this review accessed the source, not a source publication date. Neither source gives a price effective/expiry date or confirms stock; the latest available page content does not establish that either offer remains current today.

Two candidates meet this bar for a **verification pilot**. Neither is eligible for matching implementation yet.

## Excluded from this pilot

- **Geely EX2 (PRO/MAX):** unusually useful local importer ficha evidence for trim-specific seats, cargo, battery and NEDC/WLTP range, plus a local price listing. The checked Uruguay product page/ficha does not state a model year; the PDF filename date is not proof of vehicle model year. Excluded until the importer confirms the year and current exact-version offer.
- **Leapmotor T03:** local page gives a price, five seats, 41.3 kWh and 280 km WLTP, but does not identify model year or exact trim; its linked brochure could not be fetched. Excluded pending local version/year and cargo/charging confirmation.
- **Wuling Mini EV, Binguo EV and Bingo S:** local pages have useful claims, but checked material does not establish model year; city-range claims lack a named test cycle and version details remain incomplete. Excluded until version/year and range-cycle evidence are confirmed.
- **Riddara RD6 / Horizon versions:** local pages advertise offers and NEDC range/battery claims, but exact model year and passenger/cargo evidence were not established. Excluded pending local version/year confirmation and a version-level seating/space source.
- **BYD New E2 X / Seagull Surf / Yuan Pro:** existing starter-catalog review records local model-page evidence, but exact version/year and applicable passenger/space facts are missing. Excluded until a local exact-year/version record is confirmed.
- **Chevrolet Spark EUV, Equinox EV and Captiva EV:** prior backlog records the displayed price validity through 2026-09-30, now expired; Equinox range labels conflict. Excluded until current price and the disputed or missing version-level facts are rechecked.
- **Other workbook candidates:** omitted where the Uruguay offer, exact BEV version/year, or primary source evidence was unclear. Hybrids and REEVs remain outside MVP scope regardless of local offer evidence.

## Implementation gate and ownership decisions

Neither candidate should enter matching yet. Before the app uses either record, a catalog owner must confirm the current new-car offer and price terms directly with the Uruguay importer, retrieve an exact local-year technical sheet, and attach evidence separately to each fact. Keep price as an observation with checked and validity dates; retain WLTP on each range value. Do not compare these WLTP claims with NEDC, CLTC, EPA, or unnamed-cycle claims as if equivalent. The product/data strategy’s proposed freshness windows and manual review process still need an assigned owner and validation; this document does not grant source reuse permission.

No candidate is claimed to fit any user's needs. Matching criteria and user requirements remain to be defined.
